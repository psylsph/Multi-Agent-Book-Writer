# The Salt-Light Keeper

A fantasy mystery told in five chapters.

## Premise

Aria Voss returns to the storm-lashed island of Hesswick to take up her late
mother's post as keeper of the Beacon - a lighthouse whose flame does not burn
fire, but memory. When the Beacon starts showing her a night her mother tried
to erase, Aria must decide whether the truth is worth drowning the island's
only light.

## Characters

- **Aria Voss** - protagonist. A 29-year-old chartmaker with salt-burned hands
  and a distrust of the sea. She left Hesswick at seventeen and swore never to
  return. Sharp, stubborn, quietly grieving.
- **Elder Marrow** - antagonist. Hesswick's stolid harbormaster, keeper of the
  island's ledgers and its silences. Believes some tides should stay turned.
- **Pip Ferrow** - supporting. A twelve-year-old beacon-hand, orphaned by the
  same storm that killed Aria's mother. Talks to the gulls; they seem to
  answer.

## World

Hesswick is a granite island of four hundred souls where the sea keeps score.
The Beacon is its heart: a lighthouse whose "flame" is woven from preserved
memories, donated by islanders each equinox. Memories burn true but fade as
they burn - what the Beacon shows one night, it may not show again. The
Wreckers' Guild tends the lens; nobody tends the keepers. Storm season runs
from the first moon of autumn until the sea freezes.

## Tone & Style

Third-person limited (Aria's viewpoint), atmospheric and restrained. Salt,
rope, lamplight. Short sentences in storms, long sentences in memory. The
island dialect drops "the" before place names ("down Beacon", "over Harbor").

## Constraints

- Aria left Hesswick at seventeen and has been gone twelve years.
- Her mother, Maren Voss, died in the equinox storm one year ago.
- The Beacon shows memories, never predictions.
- Elder Marrow knew Maren; the extent of what he knew unfolds slowly.
- Pip is never a witness to the climax.

## Outline

- Chapter 1: Homecoming - Aria arrives on Hesswick for her mother's
  bequeathal and learns the Beacon will go dark unless a keeper takes the post
  by the equinox.
- Chapter 2: What the Flame Remembers - Aria accepts the post temporarily and
  sees her first memory in the flame: a night from her childhood she cannot
  place, with a second child in it who should not be there.
- Chapter 3: The Ledgers - Aria confronts Elder Marrow about the beacons'
  donation ledgers and finds a page cut out; Pip tells her about the storm
  orphan lists.
- Chapter 4: The Cut Page - Aria reconstructs the missing page and learns the
  memory she saw was donated by her mother - a confession disguised as a gift.
- Chapter 5: Keeping the Light - Storm season breaks; Marrow tries to stop the
  Beacon burning the confession. Aria chooses to let the island see the truth,
  and takes up the post for good.
